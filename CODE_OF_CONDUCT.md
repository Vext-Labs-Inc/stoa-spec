# Code of Conduct

The Stoa community is open to anyone. Participation requires only that you treat other contributors with respect.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Stoa project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Disagreeing with ideas, not people

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub repos, discussions, issues, PRs, project events, and any communication channel where you represent the Stoa project.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to **conduct@tryvext.com** (pre-handoff) or to the foundation TSC (post-handoff). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
